Showing samples on camera

  • Join from your phone tooOpen the same Meet on your phone as a second device and point it at the sample. Its camera beats any laptop webcam.
  • Soft, even lightDaylight or a soft lamp facing the sample shows true color and grain.
  • Hold it stillVideo blurs moving texture. Pause on each sample for a few seconds.
  • Follow up with a photoVideo shifts color slightly; send a still photo of the finish after the call.
