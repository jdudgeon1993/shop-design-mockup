How it works

No showroom.
No guesswork.

Buying cabinets online should feel as safe as buying them in person. So nothing goes to the manufacturer until someone who knows cabinets has signed off on it.

  1. Pick your look

    A maker, a door style and a finish. See it in real photos, not just a swatch.

  2. Build it or choose a set

    Add cabinets in the DIY Shop, or start from a curated collection that already fits together.

  3. A designer reviews every line

    Sizes, fillers, hinges and finish, checked by a person. If something won't work, we catch it before it's ordered.

  4. Ordered and on its way

    We place the order with the manufacturer and keep you posted until it arrives.

Good questions

Before you start

Do I need to know cabinet sizes?

No. Start with a curated collection or meet a designer. If you use the DIY Shop, a designer still checks every size before anything is ordered.

Can I change a curated collection?

Yes. Ask for a change and a designer adjusts the drawing and the cabinet list for you, so you never have to work out part numbers.

When will I see pricing?

Pricing is coming soon. In the meantime, a designer can walk you through your project and options.

Do I have to visit a showroom?

Never. Video consultations happen from wherever you are: no downloads and no account needed.

Not sure where to start?

Talk it through with a designer.

A short video call, your questions answered, and a clear next step. No pressure and nothing to install.
